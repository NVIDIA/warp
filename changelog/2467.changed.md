Build Warp's native libraries with `-Wall -Wextra` in addition to the existing `-Werror` on GCC and Clang. If a newer compiler
reports new warnings when building from source, pass `--no-warnings-as-errors` to `build_lib.py` or
`-DWARP_WARNINGS_AS_ERRORS=OFF` to CMake. Kernels compiled at runtime are not affected.
