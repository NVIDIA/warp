**Breaking:** Initialized Warp array subclasses now pass their own native
descriptor like their parent, so Tape gradients and `retain_grad` follow the
parent and implicit dtype/shape reinterpretation is rejected. See the
[array subclass guide](https://nvidia.github.io/warp/latest/user_guide/compatibility.html#array-subclasses)
and its migration notes for requirements, behavior changes, and conversions.
