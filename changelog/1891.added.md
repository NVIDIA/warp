Add block-Jacobi preconditioning for `warp.sparse.BsrMatrix` inputs with square blocks using
`warp.optim.linear.preconditioner(A, "block_jacobi")`. The preconditioner applies the inverse of
each symmetric positive-definite diagonal block instead of treating its scalar diagonal entries
independently. This accounts for coupling within each block. Choose a specific factorization
strategy with `"block_jacobi_tile"` (GPU-tile-parallel Cholesky; fastest to apply of the three
strategies at every block size benchmarked, 2 through 16, but requires blocks to be `float32`
or `float64` and genuinely symmetric positive-definite), `"block_jacobi_direct"` (dense QR
inverse), or `"block_jacobi_sequential"` (LDL^T factorization); the latter two are not
performance-optimized choices, but support any Warp floating scalar type including `float16`
and are zero-safe on singular/non-SPD blocks (falling back to the identity for that block
instead of producing NaNs). `"block_jacobi_direct"` additionally falls back to
`"block_jacobi_tile"` (with a warning) for block sizes above 8, since its QR kernel's one-time
compile cost grows sharply with block size. `"block_jacobi_auto"` picks a strategy by block size
(`"direct"` for 2-6, `"sequential"` for 7-11, `"tile"` for 12 and up) favoring dtype/robustness
coverage over raw speed; `"block_jacobi"` is an alias for `"block_jacobi_auto"`.
