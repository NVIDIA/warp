Fix `wp.jax_callable()` with `graph_mode=wp.JaxCallableGraphMode.WARP_STAGED` or `WARP_STAGED_EX` when the same call runs on several CUDA devices in one process, such as under `jax.shard_map` or `jax.pmap`. Calls on other devices replayed the graph and staging buffers captured on the first device and failed with CUDA errors. Each device now captures its own graph.
