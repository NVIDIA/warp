Fix `wp.tile_sort()` faulting or returning incorrectly sorted keys and values on CUDA when `block_dim` is not a multiple
of 32, or for tiles of more than 2,048 32-bit keys when the warp count does not divide 16, such as with a `block_dim` of
96, 160, or 1024. A `block_dim` that is not a multiple of 32 now uses a shared-memory sort that may be slower. Tiles of
more than 2,048 32-bit keys also no longer fail a device-side assertion on CUDA when `wp.config.mode` is `"debug"`.
