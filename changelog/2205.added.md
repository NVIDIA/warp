Add `warp.optim.linear.KrylovSolve`, a prepared solve of one system for several right-hand sides with `cg()`, `cr()`, `bicgstab()` or `gmres()`, selected by `method="cg"`, `"cr"`, `"bicgstab"` or `"gmres"`, from zero or a warm start. It keeps optional per-RHS and cumulative diagnostics on device, supports CUDA graph replay, mixed working precision for sparse matrices, and `residual="recurrence"` or `"fresh"` to choose recurrence or recomputed residuals. `warp.optim.linear.MultiRHSOperator` repeats one operator over independent right-hand sides. Linear solver states expose the squared right-hand-side norms of their latest solve as `rhs_norm_sq`. `warp.sparse.BsrMatrix.mean_row_count_sync()` returns the mean number of active blocks per row.
