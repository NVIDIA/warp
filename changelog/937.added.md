Add a `precision` keyword to `wp.tile_matmul()`. Passing `precision="tf32"` with
`float32` tiles uses TF32 input precision with `float32` accumulation. Accuracy
and performance depend on the workload and tile configuration; Tensor Core use
also depends on the GEMM configuration. The option is honored by the cuBLASDx GEMM
on CUDA compile targets of compute capability 8.0 or higher; otherwise Warp
computes in `float32` and logs a warning when the module is compiled.
