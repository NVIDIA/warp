Fix tiled BVH queries (`wp.tile_bvh_query_aabb()`, `wp.tile_bvh_query_ray()`) silently dropping results when the
kernel is launched with a `block_dim` that is not a power of two. The parallel expansion rounded the lane count up
to a power of two, so the traversal paths belonging to the missing lanes were never walked. The lane count is now
rounded down, which keeps every enumerated path owned by a live lane; the remaining lanes take their work from the
traversal stack as before.
