Fix `wp.tile_sum()`, `wp.tile_min()`, `wp.tile_max()`, `wp.tile_argmin()` and `wp.tile_argmax()` reading
uninitialized locals in their block-combine step. Five variables were assigned only under a condition and
then read unconditionally, which is undefined behavior; they are now initialized. The observable effect was
`wp.tile_sum()` returning garbage on AMDGPU whenever `block_dim` exceeded the warp width.
