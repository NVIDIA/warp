Fix `wp.device_from_jax()` and `wp.device_to_jax()` raising errors or returning another process's device or the wrong
local GPU in multi-process programs that use `jax.distributed.initialize()`. GPUs are now matched by their
process-local ordinal (`jax.Device.local_hardware_id`) instead of their global `jax.Device.id`, `wp.device_to_jax()`
returns only devices of the current process, and `wp.device_from_jax()` raises a `RuntimeError` for a GPU that belongs
to another process.
