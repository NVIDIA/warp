Fix `wp.sparse.bsr_compress()` running once at record time instead of on every replay in CPU graph captures. Replayed
matrices stayed uncompressed or got wrong values, and `fem.integrate()` or `fem.interpolate()` with
`bsr_options={"construction": "row_compress"}` or `"auto"` could crash during capture. Graphs saved with
`wp.capture_save()` now include the compression. This changes the `.wrp` format to version 19; recapture graphs saved
with earlier formats.
