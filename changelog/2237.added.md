Add `wp.tile_lu()` for square `float32` and `float64` tiles, with partial pivoting, packed factors, row-swap indices, and singularity status. Use cuSOLVERDx on supported CUDA builds and a cooperative native implementation on CPU or when MathDx solvers are disabled. Support automatic differentiation of the packed factors on both backends with the recorded pivot permutation held fixed; pivot indices and status are nondifferentiable.
