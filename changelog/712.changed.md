Speed up stores of 16-byte and larger array elements, such as `wp.vec4`, `wp.quat`, `wp.mat44`, and `wp.spatial_vector`, and loads of 16-byte elements with 4-byte components, such as `wp.vec4` and `wp.quat`, on CUDA devices by accessing them as 8- or 16-byte words when the array is aligned. Misaligned arrays, such as some PyTorch views, keep the previous element accesses. Set `wp.config.enable_wide_array_access = False` to opt out.
