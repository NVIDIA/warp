Add `warp.geometry.sparse_marching_cubes`, which extracts an isosurface directly from an implicit function (a
`wp.func` or a batched Python callable) without building a dense grid, so cost scales with surface area rather than
volume and the whole pipeline stays on the GPU. The two stages are also available separately:
`warp.geometry.lipschitz_octree` selects the cells near the level set, and
`warp.geometry.sparse_marching_cubes_from_cells` extracts a mesh from an explicit list of cells and their sampled
corner values. See `warp/examples/core/example_sparse_marching_cubes.py`.
