Add `warp.geometry.sparse_marching_cubes`, `warp.geometry.lipschitz_octree`, and
`warp.geometry.sparse_marching_cubes_from_cells` to extract isosurfaces from an implicit function -- a batched
callable `evaluate(points: wp.array[wp.vec3]) -> wp.array[wp.float32]` -- without building a dense grid, so cost
scales with surface area rather than volume. `sparse_marching_cubes_from_cells` supports backward-mode automatic
differentiation with respect to `corner_values`, and `sparse_marching_cubes` inherits it through `field`;
`lipschitz_octree`'s cell selection is not differentiated. See the
[Sparse Marching Cubes documentation](https://nvidia.github.io/warp/latest/user_guide/runtime.html#sparse-marching-cubes)
for usage.
