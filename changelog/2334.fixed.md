Fix float16 and bfloat16 `wp.svd3()` and `wp.eig3()` and their gradients, which returned wrong decompositions (including negative singular values) for float16 matrices with entries above about 100 or below about 0.01, were imprecise in bfloat16, and did not compile for bfloat16 on CUDA. They now compute in float32, as `wp.svd2()` does.
