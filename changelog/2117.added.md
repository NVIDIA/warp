Add constrained function spaces to `warp.fem`. `warp.fem.make_conforming_basis_space()` eliminates the hanging nodes of a continuous Lagrange basis of any degree on `AdaptiveNanogrid`, so that fields stay continuous across refinement levels, and reduces Dirichlet projectors and values assembled on the raw basis to its independent nodes. `warp.fem.periodic_prolongation()` ties each node that has a copy shifted by one of the given translations to that copy, `warp.fem.make_constrained_basis_space()` builds a basis from any scalar polynomial basis and a sparse prolongation matrix, and `warp.fem.hanging_node_prolongation()` exposes the hanging-node constraints so that periodicity combines with hanging-node elimination. See `example_periodic_homogenization.py`.
