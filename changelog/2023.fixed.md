Fix attribute access such as `t.q` or `v.x` on vector, quaternion, transform, and struct values captured from
Python, such as module-level globals, closure variables, or `wp.static()` results, inside kernels and functions.
The attribute was ignored and the whole value was used instead, which could silently produce wrong results, for
example `T.q[0]` returning the first translation component of a captured transform `T`.
