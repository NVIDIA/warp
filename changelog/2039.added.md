Add `warp.optim.linear.FSAI`, an adaptive factorized sparse approximate inverse preconditioner for
symmetric positive-definite `warp.sparse.BsrMatrix` inputs with `float16`, `float32` or `float64`
entries. It is also available as `warp.optim.linear.preconditioner(A, "fsai")`. With
`reuse_pattern=True`, `FSAI.update()` refits the factor after the matrix values change, and can be
captured in a CUDA graph together with the solve; `FSAI.update_status` reports on the device
whether a captured update was accepted. `regularize_refit=True` keeps refitting the other rows when
one row's local factorization fails, `validate_topology=False` skips the topology comparison, and
`defer_pattern=True` builds the preconditioner from the matrix topology alone and selects the
supports on the device during the first update, so that step can also be captured in a CUDA graph.
