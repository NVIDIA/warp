Fix the sides of `warp.fem.AdaptiveNanogrid` between cells of different levels when the grid is not graded or has missing cells. Sides took the level of the coarser neighbor, so they could overlap or extend beyond the finer cell, which inflated integrals over `Sides` and `BoundarySides` (for instance, the boundary area of a coarse cell next to a single fine cell was 37 instead of 28). Each side now covers exactly the facets of its neighbors.
