Speed up the ``wp.Mesh`` closest-point queries: ``wp.mesh_query_point()``, ``wp.mesh_query_point_no_sign()``, ``wp.mesh_query_point_sign_parity()``, and ``wp.mesh_query_point_sign_winding_number()`` now share a single shrinking-radius traversal that computes child bounds distances at the parent and carries them on the traversal stack, so each BVH node is loaded only once and nodes pruned by the shrinking search radius are discarded without reloading them. All mesh point-query variants, including ``wp.mesh_query_point_sign_normal()`` and ``wp.mesh_query_furthest_point_no_sign()``, now also read triangle vertex positions through the read-only data path.
