Reject NanoVDB index grids in `wp.Volume.load_from_nvdb()` whose on-index prefix sums don't match the leaf value masks,
or whose leaf offsets map voxels past `Volume.get_voxel_count()`.
`wp.volume_sample_index()`, `wp.volume_sample_grad_index()`, and `wp.volume_lookup_index()`
could otherwise return wrong or out-of-bounds indices into per-voxel arrays.
Grids that also assign indices to tile values or per-node statistics, which NanoVDB's grid builder can add,
may exceed the voxel count and now fail to load.
Index grids with more than `2**31 - 1` indexable voxels now fail to load with an error naming that limit.
Voxel indices past the limit, such as those of regular grids with more than about 4.2 million leaves,
now sample as the background value and look up as `-1` instead of wrapping around.
